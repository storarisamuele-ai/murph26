SECT. 01 — REF JTO-01

Mission Overview

Objective: Define the operational parameters of the Murph workout and establish a common understanding for all operators.

Murph is a Hero WOD performed in memory of Lieutenant Michael P. Murphy. The protocol consists of a one-mile run, one hundred pull-ups, two hundred push-ups, three hundred air squats, and a final one-mile run, performed in that exact sequence. Operators with the required capacity may execute the mission wearing a twenty-pound weighted vest or body armor.

SECT. 02 — REF JTO-02

Mission Origin

This Mission takes its designation from Navy Lieutenant Michael P. Murphy, who gave his life during Operation Red Wings in Afghanistan on 28 June 2005. Posthumously awarded the Medal of Honor, he was killed in action while attempting to save his team. The workout known as Murph was his preferred training protocol and is now performed worldwide to honor his sacrifice and the service of all fallen operators.

Mission Objective

Objective: Complete the workout as a deliberate act of remembrance and self-discipline.

Participation is not a race against other operators. It is a measured effort to honor Lieutenant Murphy, test individual resolve, and support the team. Every repetition is executed with intent. Every member of the unit is accountable for executing to standard.

SECT. 04 — REF JTO-04

Workout Structure

The execution sequence is fixed and does not change between operators or missions.

  1. Run — One mile to open the protocol.
  2. Pull-ups — One hundred repetitions.
  3. Push-ups — Two hundred repetitions.
  4. Air Squats — Three hundred repetitions.
  5. Run — One mile to close the protocol.

The middle three movements may be partitioned according to the authorized standard selected for the mission, but the listed order and total volume are never altered.

SECT. 05 — REF JTO-05

Authorized Formats

Murph26 authorizes the following operational formats for Mission Alpha:

  • Solo — One operator completes the full mission independently.
  • Team — Multiple operators share the workload as a unit.
  • Relay — Operators rotate through movements in sequence.
  • 4 Missions to Murph — A multi-phase training progression leading to the final event.

SECT. 06 — REF JTO-06

Authorized Standards

Every operator must select the standard that matches their current level and mission role. All standards are legitimate when executed with full range of motion and intent.

Alpha
Prescribed: full volume, with a twenty-pound weighted vest or body armor.
Bravo
Prescribed: full volume, no additional load.
Charlie
Scaled: full volume, partitioned and performed with modifications.
Delta
Scaled: reduced volume and modified movements, no load.

Selection is the operator's responsibility. Choose the protocol that allows consistent execution from start to finish.

SECT. 07 — REF JTO-07

Mission Success

Objective: Conclude the mission with integrity and respect for the standard.

Mission success is not determined by time or scoreboard placement. An operator succeeds by completing every authorized repetition, honoring the name of the workout, and finishing the protocol without shortcuts. The mission belongs to the athlete who owns every rep.

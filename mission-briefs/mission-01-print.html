Mission Objective

Objective: Complete the 10 km row and burpee protocol as a single unit of effort, maintaining integrity across every repetition and transition.

Mission Alpha is designed to introduce operators to the Murph mindset: sustained output, controlled transitions, and team accountability. The mission is not a race. It is a measured test of pacing and execution.

SECT. 04 — REF JTO-04

Execution Protocol

  1. Operators row the confirmed 10 km distance as the primary movement.
  2. Burpees are executed at the authorized intervals once the final prescription is released.
  3. Work may be partitioned or shared according to the selected format and standard.
  4. Each operator is responsible for counting every repetition and executing to range of motion.
  5. Team execution follows the format-specific distribution rules.

The exact sequence is fixed and will not change after the final order is issued.

SECT. 06 — REF JTO-06

Movement Standards

Final movement standards will be issued with the mission prescription. The following baseline expectations apply:

  • Row: maintain a consistent stroke rate and full range of motion for the full 10 km.
  • Burpee: chest to floor, jump to a standing finish. Scale versions will be defined in the final order.
  • Transitions: move directly from the rower to the burpee station without unnecessary rest.

Scaled standards exist to keep every operator in the mission. No shortcut is authorized.

SECT. 07 — REF JTO-07

Mission Notes

  • Safety: monitor your pace and breathing. Row 10 km at a sustainable effort.
  • Equipment: rower, floor space for burpees, hydration, towel.
  • Strategy: do not sprint the opening meters. The mission is decided in the final kilometers.
  • Integrity: every rep counts. Count every rep.

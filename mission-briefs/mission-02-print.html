MISSION EXECUTION

Cargo Carry

  • 4 Cargo Items per Team.
  • Distribution is completely free.
  • One athlete may carry one or more Cargo Items.
  • Carriers may change at any time.
  • The same distribution does not need to be maintained.
  • All 4 Cargo Items must advance with the Team.

Team Run — Corsa del Montecucco

The distance is not scaled. All Teams run the same route and must complete it together. The route will be guided by Staff on bicycles.

ALTERNATE MISSION

An athlete who does not run stays at the Base and performs an Alternate Mission until the Team returns. The modification is independent of the Cargo Standard. The machine can be chosen based on the athlete.

4 Deadlift + Assault Bike / SkiErg

Every 3:00, including 0:00. Continue until the Team returns.

Deadlift Standard — The load must allow 4 unbroken reps, technically sound and without grinding. If not possible, use the lower Standard.

Mission Termination

Performance — When the Team returns, the athlete rejoins the Team for the final Cargo Carry.

Fitness — When the Team returns, the athlete rejoins the Team for the final Cargo Carry.

CrossFit — When the Team returns, the Mission ends immediately.

Intensity

Aerobic / Continuous / Intentional — RPE 6–7 / 10

No Erg meters or calories are scored. The goal is not to maximize output, but to maintain real aerobic work.

Sustainable doesn't mean comfortable.

MISSION BRIEF #02

MISSION BRAVO

SECT. 07 — REF JTO-07

MISSION HAZARDS

  • ! Pacing — Bravo arrives when it is still easy to push too hard. Do not turn the Team Run into a time trial.
  • ! Team Separation — Nobody gets left behind. The Team must move as a single unit.
  • ! Grip Fatigue — Do not wait for grip failure. Frequent exchanges preserve grip and forearms.
  • ! Uneven Terrain — Part of the run takes place on the Montecucco Park trails. Watch for ground, turns and other users. Safety > Pace.
  • ! Heat & Hydration — Show up hydrated. Begin recovery immediately after the Mission.
  • ! Alternate Mission Intensity — Bike and SkiErg are not a recovery station. RPE 6–7. Deadlifts should not become maximal strength work.

SECT. 08 — REF JTO-08

MISSION INTEL

THE FASTEST ATHLETE DOESN'T SET THE PACE.
THE TEAM DOES.

BRAVO È LA MISSIONE PIÙ LUNGA DELLA GIORNATA. NON DEVE DIVENTARE LA PIÙ DURA.

Strategic Intel

Cargo
Do not wait for the carrier to be exhausted. Plan exchanges.
Run
Quickly identify the sustainable pace of the entire Team.
Alternate Mission
The Erg must remain aerobic. Deadlifts are checkpoints, not mini-workouts.
Return to Base
Performance and Fitness: the Mission is not over at the end of the run.
